The Studio

Imagify is a visual studio in Kuala Lumpur working across identity, image and film. We like briefs with edges, budgets with honesty and rooms with daylight, which in this city means shooting before the four o'clock rain. Most of our projects begin as a conversation about what a brand refuses to do, because the refusals draw the shape faster than the wishes, and from that outline we build systems that stay calm in use and sharp in memory. The studio is small on purpose, one floor of a shophouse off Jalan Mesui. Everyone who starts a project finishes it, and the person who presents the work is the person who made it.
