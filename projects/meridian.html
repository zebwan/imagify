Glass Season
Design
2026

Glass Season was a design programme for a glassware maker, and we rebuilt the catalogue system around refraction, letting each object distort a coloured field behind it so the products drew their own portraits, and the typography stayed small and factual beneath these distortions, and the system carried through packaging, fair stands and a slim annual publication.
