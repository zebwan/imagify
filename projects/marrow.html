Bone China
Identity
2025

Bone China was an identity for a small porcelain house, and we drew the mark from the thinnest point of their cups, a line that almost disappears against light, and the system used that near-absence everywhere, hairline rules, pale embossing and photography shot against white so the objects had to define themselves by shadow alone, and the effect made thinness itself the brand.
