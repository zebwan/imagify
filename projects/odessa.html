Red Interval
Editorial
2025

Red Interval was a colour-themed editorial where every spread had to earn its single red element, and we built each story around waiting, queues, platforms and pauses, with red arriving as a coat, a door or a signal lamp, and the type sat in the intervals between images, and the issue closed with the only fully red spread, printed on uncoated stock.
