Paloma

Street Chorus
Photography
2025

Street Chorus followed a dance company through their own city, and instead of stages we shot rehearsals in crossings, markets and underpasses, letting passers-by drift through frame, and the photography leaned on long lenses and found light so the dancers stayed inside the life of the street rather than on top of it, and the final selection ran as a broadsheet insert.
