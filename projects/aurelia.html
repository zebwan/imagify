Slow Bloom
Advertising
2026

Slow Bloom advertised a fragrance built on serai wangi and frangipani by refusing to hurry, and the films held single flowers opening in near real time while the bottle appeared only in the final second, and the stills used the same patience, printed large with almost nothing on the page, and the media plan placed the work in clinic waiting rooms, LRT platforms and hotel lobbies along Jalan Sultan Ismail, exactly where people had time to watch something bloom.
