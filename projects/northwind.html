Salt Air
Creative Direction
2026

Salt Air set the direction for a coastal outerwear line, and we built the visual world around wind rather than water, letting fabric, hair and grass all lean the same way in every frame, and the campaign grammar used long horizons with the subject placed low, so the clothing read as shelter inside weather, and every touchpoint from lookbook to signage kept that same leaning horizon.
