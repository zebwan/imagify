Dust Ballet
Film Direction
2024

Dust Ballet was a short film for a heritage denim house, and we choreographed riders, dust and low sun into a single continuous take, rehearsed for three days and shot at the fourth dusk, and the fabric was the lead actor, catching light through the haze, and the film ran in one cinema for one week, which was the whole strategy, and the queue did the advertising.
